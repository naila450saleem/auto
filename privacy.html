Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2025

At Expedite Auto Transportation, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, email address, and shipping information when you request a quote or book a service.
  • Automatic Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage through cookies or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage auto transportation services.

  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.

You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying "STOP" to SMS messages or contacting us at Sales@expediteautotransportation.com.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your data may only be shared with:

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.

To exercise your rights, contact Sales@expediteautotransportation.com.

  • By opting in, you agree to receive SMS messages regarding quotes, updates, or promotions.

  • Reply "STOP" to unsubscribe or "HELP" for assistance.

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing your information.
